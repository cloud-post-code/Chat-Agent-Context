Why titanium rivets fatigue differently

By Priya Raman · May 14, 2026

Titanium's fatigue curve flattens where aluminum's keeps falling, which changes how you should size a riveted lap joint. In our bench tests a Ti-6Al-4V rivet survived ten million cycles at loads that cracked 5056 aluminum heads inside two million. The catch is galvanic pairing: put titanium against bare aluminum skin and the joint corrodes from the inside out unless you isolate it.
